Kairos in 30 seconds

Every part of Kairos. In half a minute.

The name

The Greeks had two words for time. Chronos is the measured kind: hours, sprints, the calendar. Kairos is the other kind, the opportune moment, the narrow opening when action works. Kairos the app is built for that: it keeps the hours of work running in the background, so you can be present for the moments that matter.

What it does in 2.0.0

One workspace. Your models, projects and memory.

01

Start with a model guide

Sign in to bundled Claude Code, install Codex with your permission, test an API key, or download a local model through Cookbook. There is no default model. Choose one for each chat.

02

A memory you own

Your knowledge is a vault of plain Markdown files. Search it, browse it as a linked map, and edit it in Kairos or any other tool. It stays useful without the app.

03

Agents that remember

Named agents with their own role, goals and memory. Admins hand work to an agent with an @ mention and receive its result in chat. Agents approve tool requests automatically within their limits and can work in teams.

04

Work on a schedule

Scheduled prompts and built-in routines like a daily brief. Each runs once, can be stopped, and delivers its result here or to a connected channel.

05

Run history

Review recorded tool calls, timings, errors and outcomes for chats, tasks and agents. Saved timelines help you follow the work.

06

Back up app data

Export chats, notes, agents and settings in one archive. Restore with a safety copy of what was there. Encrypted credentials and their key are left out unless you choose to include them.

Before you install

The installers aren't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper may ask you to approve the app the first time. The Mac build runs on Apple Silicon, not Intel Macs. Screenshots use demo data.

Kairos runs on your computer. When you choose a cloud model or connect an outside service, the data that request needs goes to that provider.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Kairos free?

Yes. Kairos is free and open source under the MIT license. Models you connect may have their own costs: a Claude or Codex subscription, or an API account. Local models cost nothing to run.

Where does my data live?

On your computer. Chats, settings and agents are in your app data folder, and your memory is a folder of Markdown files you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you connect a cloud model or an outside service.

Which models work?

Claude through Claude Code, Codex through the Codex CLI, local models through Ollama or the built-in engine, and any OpenAI-compatible API. You can switch per conversation.

Does it update itself?

On Windows, yes: updates download in the background and install when you quit. On macOS, download the new version from this page and replace the app.

Can I reach it from my phone?

Yes, through Tailscale, set up in Settings. It's never exposed to the public internet: it serves HTTPS on your private network and requires a login.

Can I back up and move my data?

Yes. Settings exports app data in one archive, and restore keeps a safety copy of what it replaces. Vault notes need their own backup. Logs and checkpoint history are left out.

What happened to JARVIS?

JARVIS is now Kairos. The update keeps your install, your chats and your settings exactly where they were. On a Mac, after installing Kairos you can delete the old JARVIS app.
